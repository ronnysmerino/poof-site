Questions

What is Poof?

Poof makes small apps for everyday things, like a water log, a tip splitter, a level or a game night score keeper. Describe what you need, by typing or speaking, and Poof makes the app in seconds. Your apps stay on your iPhone, ready to open again.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Making apps from your own words needs Apple Intelligence, turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri, on an iPhone that supports it. Without it, Poof makes apps from its built-in designs.

What is Poof Pro?

Poof is free to try, with example apps and room for 3 apps you make (the examples don't count). Poof Pro is a one-time purchase that keeps as many apps as you make. It works with Family Sharing. Without Pro, you can always delete one of your apps to make room for a new one.

How do I restore Poof Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Poof, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What is Faster Understanding?

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Poof downloads a small helper from Apple once (about 1 GB), on Wi-Fi, so it understands what you ask more quickly. It runs only on your iPhone. You can see its progress, download it now on cellular, or turn it off, in Poof's Settings › Faster Understanding. Poof works fully before it arrives.

How do I share an app?

Touch and hold an app in My Apps, then tap Share App to send it by AirDrop, Messages or another app. It goes without anything you put in it. When someone shares an app with you, Poof asks before adding it.

Poof says it can't make this one yet. What now?

Some things are beyond what Poof can make so far, like controlling your TV. Poof tells you instead of making the wrong app. Tap Make Something Close to get the nearest app it can make, or Change My Words to try describing it another way.

An app came out wrong, or I saw something offensive

Touch and hold the app and tap Report a Problem. It opens an email to us with the app's name and what you asked for, never what you put in it. Poof is an AI, so it can make mistakes; double-check anything important.

How do I delete my data?

Everything stays on your iPhone. To remove what's in an app, touch and hold it and tap Clear Data. To remove the app, tap Delete. Deleting Poof from your iPhone removes everything. There's no account and nothing to delete on our side.

Can I use Poof for my health?

Mini apps can read and save the Apple Health data you choose, like steps, sleep, water or cycle days, after you allow it. They're for personal use and aren't medical advice. Talk to a doctor about medical decisions.
