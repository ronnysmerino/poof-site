How it works

No templates to fill in and nothing to set up. You describe it, Poof makes it.

  1. Step 1

    Describe it

    Type or say what you need, in your own words.

  2. Step 2

    Use it right away

    Poof makes the app on your iPhone, ready to use.

  3. Step 3

    Keep it

    Every app you make is saved in My Apps for next time.

Things you can ask for

Everyday helpers, not big projects. A few ideas to start with.

Track

  • “A water log with a reminder”
  • “Habits with streaks”
  • “When did I last water each plant?”

Work it out

  • “Split this receipt by who ordered what”
  • “Who owes what after the trip”
  • “Cost per mile for my road trip”

Camera and sensors

  • “Count the coins in this photo”
  • “Count my squats with the camera”
  • “Tell me when the baby cries”

Plan and play

  • “A focus timer, 25 on and 5 off”
  • “A packing list for the beach”
  • “Keep score for game night”

Works like the rest of your iPhone

The apps you make aren't stuck inside Poof.

Siri

Widgets

Lock Screen

Apple Health
Read and save steps, sleep, water and more, after you allow it.
Change it by asking
Want it different? For many apps, just tell Poof.
Share with a friend
