Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026. The same policy is in the app, under Settings › Privacy Policy.

The short version

Poof has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and we never collect, see, or sell your data. The apps you build, and what you put in them, stay on your iPhone.

What you describe

Apple Intelligence turns your description into an app. When Apple's Private Cloud Compute is available, Poof may send your description there to use a larger model; Private Cloud Compute is built so a request is used only to answer it and isn't stored or made available to anyone, Apple included. Otherwise it's handled by the model on your iPhone. Features inside your apps, like chat and photo analysis, always run on your iPhone.

Permissions

Poof asks for the camera, photos, microphone, speech recognition, location, contacts, calendars, reminders, or motion only when you dictate an idea or a mini-app feature needs it, and you can change each one in Settings. What the camera, photos, and microphone capture is processed on your iPhone, and dictation is transcribed on your iPhone too. Poof never records audio to disk and never keeps a copy of your contacts.

Faster Understanding

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Poof downloads a small language model from Apple once, on Wi-Fi unless you tap Download Now. It runs only on your iPhone and helps Poof understand what you ask; nothing you type is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings.

Live weather, maps, and exchange rates

Mini-apps that show nearby places or weather send the city or location they need to Apple Maps or Apple Weather (WeatherKit), including periodic background forecast refreshes when you set up a weather alert. Currency converter mini-apps fetch daily exchange rates for the requested base currency from ExchangeRate-API's public feed without sending any account, personal, or device identifiers.

Apple Health

A mini-app reads Apple Health data only after you tap Sync Apple Health, and saves to it only when you log something. Health data stays on your iPhone: Poof doesn't send it anywhere, store it in iCloud, share it, or use it for advertising. Mini-apps that track health are for personal use, not medical advice.

Sharing

When you share from a mini-app, with a QR code, AirDrop, or another app, it goes only where you send it.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page will say so. For questions, email ronnysmerino@gmail.com.
